A small workspace can still feel spacious. The key is to make every item earn its place.

## Keep the essentials close

Start with the things you use every day: your computer, notebook, pen, and a light source. Move occasional-use items into a drawer or shelf.

![A tidy home office desk](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1497366754035-f200968a6e72?w=1200)

## Use vertical space

A wall shelf or pegboard can free up valuable desk space. Keep the surface as clear as possible so you have room to work.

## Make it yours

A plant, a print, or a favorite object can make the corner feel welcoming without adding clutter.
