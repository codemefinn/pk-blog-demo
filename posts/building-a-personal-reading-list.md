A reading list is most useful when it makes you excited to read. It does not need to be long or perfectly organized.

## Mix different kinds of books

Add a novel, a practical guide, an essay collection, or something completely outside your usual interests. Variety keeps the list inviting.

![Books stacked on a table](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512820790803-83ca734da794?w=1200)

## Keep the list easy to update

A simple note on your phone or a small notebook is enough. Include the title and one sentence about why it caught your attention.

## Read what fits the moment

You do not have to finish books in the order you added them. Follow your curiosity and let the list change over time.
