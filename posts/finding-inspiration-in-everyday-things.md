Inspiration is not always a dramatic moment. It can be a color combination on a shopfront, a sentence overheard on a train, or the shape of a shadow.

## Collect small observations

Keep a note for phrases, images, colors, and ideas that catch your attention. Do not worry about organizing them immediately.

![Sunlight falling across a landscape](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1470252649378-9c29740c9fa8?w=1200)

## Follow unexpected connections

Look through your notes every now and then. An observation from one day might become the starting point for a completely different project.

## Stay curious

Visit a place you have never been, read about an unfamiliar subject, or ask someone about their work. New inputs give your ideas more room to grow.
