A blog does not have to become a publication with a strict schedule. It can simply be a place to think out loud and keep a record of what matters to you.

## A home for your ideas

Short notes, longer essays, tutorials, and project updates can live together. Over time, they become a record of your interests and experiments.

![A notebook open beside a laptop](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1456324504439-367cee3b3c32?w=1200)

## Share what you learn

Writing an explanation often helps you understand a subject more clearly. It may also help someone else who is trying to solve the same problem.

## Let it evolve

Your blog can change as your interests change. There is no need to decide what it must become before you begin.
