A good morning does not need to be complicated. A few small habits can help you begin the day with a clearer head.

## Start before the screens

Instead of reaching for your phone immediately, give yourself a few quiet minutes. Open the curtains, drink some water, and let the day begin at its own pace.

![A warm cup of coffee on a quiet morning](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1495474472287-4d71bcdd2085?w=1200)

## Choose one priority

Write down the one thing that would make today feel productive. You can still have a long to-do list, but one clear priority gives your attention somewhere to land.

## Keep it realistic

A routine should support your life, not become another task to maintain. Start with ten minutes and adjust as you go.
