Planning a week should make life easier, not fill every available hour. A little structure can create more room to breathe.

## Choose three priorities

Look at the week ahead and identify three outcomes that matter most. Add smaller tasks only after those priorities have a place.

![A notebook calendar and weekly planning notes](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1506784983877-45594efa4cbe?w=1200)

## Leave some empty space

Avoid scheduling every hour. Open time gives you room to rest, handle surprises, and follow ideas that come up during the week.

## Review and adjust

At the end of the week, notice what worked and what felt crowded. Use that information to make the next plan more realistic.
