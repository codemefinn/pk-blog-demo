You do not always need a destination for a good walk. Sometimes the best plan is to choose a direction and see what catches your attention.

## Take the quieter route

Try a street you usually pass by, or turn into a neighborhood park. A different route can make a familiar city feel new.

![A city street in soft afternoon light](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1519608487953-e999c86e7455?w=1200)

## Notice the details

Look for interesting architecture, small gardens, old signs, and local shops. Take a photo if something makes you pause.

## Leave room for a detour

A walk becomes more enjoyable when you are not watching the clock. Stop for a drink, browse a bookstore, or simply sit for a while.
