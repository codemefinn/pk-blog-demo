Indoor plants can bring a little life into a room. You do not need a large collection to get started.

## Match the plant to the light

Spend a day noticing how sunlight moves through your room. Choose a plant that suits the conditions you actually have.

![Green indoor plants by a window](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1485955900006-10f4d324d411?w=1200)

## Avoid overwatering

Check the soil before watering instead of following a rigid schedule. Different pots, rooms, and seasons change how quickly soil dries.

## Start with one

Choose one easy-care plant and learn what it needs. Once you understand its rhythm, adding another will feel much simpler.
