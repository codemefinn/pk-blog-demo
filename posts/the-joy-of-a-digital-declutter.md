Digital clutter is easy to accumulate because it does not take up physical space. A short reset can make your devices feel calmer.

## Start with your desktop

Move loose files into a few clearly named folders. Delete duplicates and archive anything you no longer need to see every day.

![A clean laptop workspace](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1498050108023-c5249f4df085?w=1200)

## Review notifications

Turn off alerts that do not need your immediate attention. Keep the ones that genuinely help you respond to people or manage important tasks.

## Close the open loops

Save useful links, bookmark pages you want to revisit, and close tabs that are no longer relevant. You can always search for them again.
