Creative work often gets pushed to the end of the day. Waiting for a perfect block of free time can mean waiting forever.

## Make the starting point smaller

Instead of planning to write for two hours, begin with fifteen minutes. Open the document, sketch one idea, or collect a few references.

![Notebook and writing tools on a desk](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1455390582262-044cdead277a?w=1200)

## Separate making from judging

Give yourself time to create a rough first version without editing every sentence or correcting every detail. Review can happen later.

## Build a gentle habit

Choose a repeatable time and make the setup easy. Consistency matters more than occasional bursts of motivation.
